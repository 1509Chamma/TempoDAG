# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive; harassment and personal attacks
are not tolerated; disagreements are resolved on technical merit, with
evidence. The project's own standard applies to discussions too — claims
come with something checkable attached.

To report unacceptable behavior, open an issue marked *conduct* or contact
the maintainer through the repository. Reports are handled discreetly.
